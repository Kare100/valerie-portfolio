2021The tidy plan

I started with the sensible degree

In 2021 I started Financial Engineering at Strathmore University. I took it because, in the eyes of my parents and everyone else, it was a good career. Safe, respectable, very grown up. Maths, markets, models.

It is a decent reason to start something. It turned out not to be a decent enough reason to finish it.

2022The wobble

Good at it, but not for life

About a year in, I was good at it and I knew exactly what it needed. I just couldn't picture doing it for the rest of my life, all the way to retirement. That is a hard thing to admit when everyone around you thinks you chose well.

Changing course felt like admitting I had wasted a year. It wasn't a waste. It was the year I found out what I was not meant for, which is half of knowing what you are. What came next took me a little longer to name, and that is allowed.

I was good at it. I just couldn't do it forever.
