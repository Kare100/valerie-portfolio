AgriSmart Kenya

A bilingual (English/Swahili) app that lets smallholder farmers photograph a sick leaf and get a diagnosis, treatment plan, market prices and weather advice, built for mid-range phones and patchy rural networks.

  • Gemini Vision pipeline with a prompt tuned for Kenyan smallholder crops, parsed into disease, confidence and numbered treatment steps
  • Compresses photos to 1024px / 70% JPEG before upload, so one diagnosis doesn't eat a farmer's data bundle
  • Fuzzy-matches AI output to a structured diseases table, with a real "this isn't a plant" path
  • Jest unit tests, an integration suite and CI on every push
  • React Native

  • PostgreSQL

About

I changed one word, and found my thing.

2021 · Strathmore University

FINANCIALFinancialENGINEERING

The safe, sensible degree. I was good at it. I just couldn't picture doing it for life.

I started in Financial Engineering at Strathmore in 2021: maths, markets, models. A year in, I knew I wasn't meant to do it forever. In Fall 2023 I switched to Software Engineering at USIU-Africa. Same second word, much better fit.

That pivot is the most useful thing I own. I pick up new stacks fast, I'm comfortable with change, and I still think like an analyst: what's the risk, what does the data say, and who gets hurt if the model is wrong?

I'm heading toward fintech, AI and ML, building systems that make good decisions with imperfect information, fairly.
