01 · featured

AgriSmart Kenya

A bilingual English/Swahili app: farmers photograph a sick leaf and get a diagnosis, a treatment plan, market prices and weather advice. Built for mid-range phones and patchy rural networks.

  • Gemini Vision pipeline tuned for Kenyan smallholder crops, parsed into disease, confidence and treatment steps
  • Photos compressed to 1024px / 70% JPEG so one diagnosis doesn't eat a farmer's data bundle
  • AI output fuzzy-matched to a structured diseases table, plus a real "this isn't a plant" path
  • Jest unit tests, an integration suite and CI on every push
  • React Native
  • Expo
  • Supabase
  • Gemini
  • PostgreSQL
  • Jest

03  About

I changed one word
and found my thing.

Dear recruiter,

In 2021 I started FinancialSoftware! Engineering at Strathmore. It was the safe, sensible choice, and I was good at it. I just couldn't picture doing it for the rest of my life.

So in Fall 2023 I switched to Software Engineering at USIU-Africa. Same second word, much better fit. I've been on the Dean's List every semester since.

That pivot taught me to learn fast and stay calm when things change. I still think like an analyst: what's the risk, what does the data say, and who gets hurt if the model is wrong? Now I'm heading toward fintech, AI and ML, building systems that decide well with imperfect information, and fairly.
